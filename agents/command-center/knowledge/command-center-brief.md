# Command Center brief

Command Center reads this before every job. It holds the settings that are only about Command
Center: how it addresses you, your daily rhythm, and what your morning brief should contain. Who you
are, how you like reports written, your working hours and the things an agent must never do
without asking live in `shared/about-me.md`, which `/onboard` fills. They are not asked again
here.

`/command-center-onboarding` fills the blanks below by interviewing you. `/customize-team` can
fill them too. You can also type over any of them yourself.

## What you call it

The name lives in its own file, `agents/command-center/knowledge/assistant-name.md`, because the
dashboard's voice assistant reads it too: one name for both, changed in one place. It is filled
there, not here.
